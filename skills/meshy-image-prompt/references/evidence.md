# Evidence: image prompts for Image to 3D

Live runs from 2026-10-07 with `nano-banana-pro` on `POST /openapi/v1/text-to-image` and `ai_model: "latest"` (Meshy 7.1) on Image to 3D, plus a comparison of six other image models on 2026-10-16. One sample per arm unless a count is given.

## Formula versus bare prompt (helmet image, 9 credits each)

| Arm | Prompt | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Bare | "a knight's helmet" | Feather plume, grey background, cast shadow, tilted pose |
| Formula | "A medieval knight's helmet, full object, three-quarter front view, centered with space around it, dark hammered iron dome with a narrow eye slit and a pointed visor, brass rivets, polished steel ridge along the top, light rust in the dents, realistic style, soft even studio lighting, plain white background, single object, no props, no text" | Three-quarter view, near-white background, no props, the requested materials |

## Pose test (dwarf blacksmith, one image per arm, then mesh-only Image to 3D)

Base prompt: "A stocky dwarf blacksmith, braided grey beard reaching the belt, leather apron over a red tunic, heavy brown boots, full body, front view, centered with space around it, stylized proportions, soft even studio lighting, plain white background, single character, no props, no text". Arms B and C appended "standing in an A-pose, arms straight and angled down about 45 degrees away from the body, hands open and clear of the body, legs slightly apart, facing forward". Arm D appended "sitting on a wooden stool, hands resting on his knees".

| Arm | Setup | Image | Image to 3D mesh |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | `pose_mode: "a-pose"`, no pose words | Clean A-pose, arms about 45 degrees from the body | Arms and hands separate from the torso |
| B | A-pose described in the prompt, no `pose_mode` | Clean A-pose | Arms separate, widest gap of the three |
| C | Both, matching | A-pose with arms higher; style and framing drifted (photoreal, smaller in frame, floor shadow) | Arms separate |
| D | `pose_mode: "a-pose"` with "sitting on a stool" | A mix: A-pose arms, straight legs, a stool between the legs | Not run |

The three meshes came out at 0.6 to 1.4 million faces and were remeshed to 30,000, retextured with the source image as `image_style_url`, and rigged. Rig results are in [rig-ready-characters.md](rig-ready-characters.md).

## Single image versus multi-view (toy robot with a battery pack and wind-up key on its back)

Prompt: "A stylized toy robot standing upright, round glass dome head, boxy teal body, short stubby legs, a large red battery pack with yellow-and-black warning stripes strapped to its back, a brass wind-up key sticking out of the back of the battery pack, single centered object, plain light-grey studio background, even soft lighting, full object visible". All 3D runs on `latest` with `multi_view_thumbnails: true`.

| Arm | Images | 3D result |
|---|---|---|
| A: single Text to Image, then Image to 3D | One 1024-square opaque image; the model drew a rear three-quarter view | The front was invented: a blob inside the dome |
| B: Image to Image with `generate_multi_view: true` from image A, then Multi-Image to 3D | Three cropped transparent PNGs, design and key orientation consistent with A | Best of the three |
| C: Text to Image with `generate_multi_view: true`, then Multi-Image to 3D | Three views that disagreed on the key's orientation | Key ended up on the side of the battery |

Multi-view Text to Image and Image to Image cost 9 credits, the same as one single image, and took 66 to 97 s against 24 s. The three views were about 340 to 450 by 610 to 690 pixels. The glass dome came out opaque white in all three arms.

## Other image models (helmet and posed dwarf, 2026-10-16)

The helmet formula prompt above with `aspect_ratio: "1:1"`, and the pose-test base prompt with `pose_mode: "a-pose"` and no pose words, run on six more models. Each posed image then went through mesh-only Image to 3D (`latest`, `should_texture: false`, `multi_view_thumbnails: true`), and the mesh was judged from its front, left, back and right thumbnails. Each mesh took 37 to 42 s. One sample per arm. nano-banana-pro is the 2026-10-07 baseline above, not rerun. nano-banana got only the helmet plus one posed image, with no mesh, because a posed nano-banana request is generated by nano-banana-pro. "Background" is the mean corner brightness, where 255 is pure white.

| Model | Helmet (credits, s) | Helmet result | Posed dwarf (credits, s) | Pose and image | Mesh |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| nano-banana-pro (baseline) | 9, 47 | Pass: three-quarter view, background 246, requested materials | 9, 32 | A-pose, hands clear; background 225, cast shadow | Arms clear |
| nano-banana | 3, 128 | Partial: soft cast shadow; brass-edged ridge instead of polished steel, rounded visor | —, 21 | Generated by `nano-banana-pro`, as documented; A-pose, hands clear; photoreal, not stylized | Not run |
| nano-banana-2 | 6, 83 | Pass with notes: every material; background 243, soft shadow, short mail fringe added | 6, 132 | A-pose, hands clear; outlined illustration style, leather cap added | Arms and hands clear, 991,726 faces |
| nano-banana-2-lite | 3, 10 | Partial: chainmail neck guard added, draped onto the floor; background 241, shadow | 3, 11 | A-pose, hands clear; photoreal; background 224, floor shadow | Arms clear, widest gap, 1,077,158 faces |
| gpt-image-2 | 9, 31 | Pass: background 254, faint contact shadow, every material | 9, 36 | A-pose with lower arms, hands clear; background 254, no shadow | Arms clear, hands nearest the hips, 1,353,064 faces |
| gpt-image-2-5-flare | 9, 19 | Pass: background 254, faint contact shadow, every material | 9, 19 | A-pose, hands clear; background 254, no shadow | Arms clear, 1,137,416 faces |
| gpt-image-2-5-sunburst | 9, 35 | Pass: background 254, no shadow, every material; rust heavier than asked | 9, 36 | A-pose, hands clear; background 254; tight crop, hair 9 px from the top | Arms clear, 1,227,994 faces |

Findings:

- `pose_mode: "a-pose"` gave a rig-ready A-pose for every model requested: 6 of 6 posed images and 5 of 5 meshes, one sample each. The GPT Image models apply it too.
- The GPT Image models followed the formula most closely: pure white backgrounds, little or no shadow, every requested material and nothing added, on both prompts. The nano-banana family, pro included, gave light grey backgrounds and soft shadows on most images.
- The 3-credit models drifted on the helmet. nano-banana dropped two requested details; nano-banana-2-lite added a dense, thin chainmail part.
- "Stylized proportions" was followed loosely: the dwarfs from nano-banana (via pro) and nano-banana-2-lite came out photoreal, and the one from nano-banana-2 as an outlined illustration.
- All four nano-banana requests with `pose_mode` returned 896×1200 (3:4). The GPT Image models returned 1024×1024 with no `aspect_ratio` set; `pose_mode` with `aspect_ratio: "3:4"` on a GPT model was not tried.
- Without remesh every mesh was 0.99 to 1.35 million faces, over the 300,000-face rigging limit.

## Props (2026-10-20)

Six props. Each prop got a bare image (the same two-to-four words as the Text to 3D bare prompts) and a formula image on `gpt-image-2-5-flare` with `aspect_ratio: "1:1"`, 9 credits each. Each formula image then went through textured Image to 3D on `latest` with `multi_view_thumbnails: true` (30 credits). The meshes were rendered from the front, a raised three-quarter angle, both sides and the back. One sample per arm.

| Prop | Bare image | Formula image | Image to 3D (faces, seconds) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lantern | "an oil lantern": a hurricane lantern with a lit flame | Square brass lantern, nearly front-on; the burner shows through clear glass | Open frame with no panes; the burner was modeled inside; loop handle intact (767,900, 85) |
| Chair | "a wooden chair": a slat-back chair | Six spindles, curved top rail, stretchers | Every spindle and stretcher, clean (135,948, 59) |
| Sword | "a sword": diagonal on a dark grey background | Vertical, point down, front-on; most of the 1:1 frame is empty | All parts, textured (47,804, 47) |
| Plant | "a potted plant": a peace lily in a grey pot | Monstera in a terracotta pot | Split leaves, stems and pot all faithful (976,164, 90) |
| Cottage | "a small house": a modern house with bushes and a potted plant | Thatched cottage, timber frame, stone chimney | Faithful, thatch included (7,693,740 faces, a 303 MB GLB, 821) |
| Van | "a cartoon car": a red car with a face | Mint-green van; the cargo box runs off the right edge | Faithful; the hidden end of the cargo box was completed plausibly (1,615,952, 152) |

Follow-up variants:

| Variant | Change | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Lantern v2 | "frosted glass panes" instead of "clear glass panes" | Solid panes with the burner's outline painted on them (384,500 faces, 50 s) |
| Van v2 | `aspect_ratio: "4:3"`, same prompt | The whole van fit in the frame, at a clearer three-quarter angle (image only) |
| Cottage remesh | Same cottage image, Image to 3D with `should_remesh: true`, `target_polycount: 30000` | 30,028 faces, a 6.6 MB GLB, 195 s, still 30 credits. At viewing size it looks like the 7.7-million-face version, because the thatch is carried by the texture. It is a new generation, so the chimney came out a little different. 812 non-manifold edges, as expected from a triangle remesh |

Formula image prompts (the van's is in SKILL.md):

- Lantern: "A Victorian oil lantern, full object, three-quarter front view, centered with space around it, square frame of aged brass with four clear glass panes, domed brass cap with chimney vents, round carry handle on top, squat brass fuel tank at the base, darker patina in the recesses, realistic style, soft even studio lighting, plain white background, single object, no props, no text"
- Sword: "A medieval longsword, full object, three-quarter front view, centered with space around it, polished steel double-edged blade with a groove down the center, straight blackened-steel crossguard, grip wrapped in dark brown leather, round brass disc pommel, realistic style, soft even studio lighting, plain white background, single object, no props, no text"
- Plant: "A potted monstera plant, full object, three-quarter front view, centered with space around it, about eight large split heart-shaped leaves on long stems, glossy deep green leaves, round tapered matte terracotta pot with a thick rim, dark soil, realistic style, soft even studio lighting, plain white background, single object, no props, no text"
- Cottage: "A small fantasy cottage, full object, three-quarter front view, centered with space around it, whitewashed walls with dark timber beams, steep thatched straw roof, round grey stone chimney, red arched wooden door, two small windows with green shutters, stone foundation, stylized style, soft even studio lighting, plain white background, single object, no props, no text"
- Chair: in SKILL.md.

Findings:

- The formula removed what the bare prompts added (a lit flame, a face, bushes, a potted plant, a grey background) in 6 of 6.
- Image to 3D followed all six formula images, including thin structural parts. On Text to 3D the same ideas lost the cartoon style, some proportions and some part counts.
- The image model did not always draw a three-quarter view: the lantern and the sword came out almost front-on. Both converted cleanly.
- Clear glass gave no panes; frosted glass gave solid panes.
- The thatched cottage was the outlier: 7.7 million faces, 303 MB and 821 s. It is one sample, so it does not show whether the thatch or the building caused it. With `should_remesh: true` and 30,000 faces, the same image gave a 6.6 MB model in 195 s that looked the same at viewing size, at the same 30 credits.



## Sources

- [Text to Image API](https://docs.meshy.ai/api/text-to-image): `ai_model` required; credits nano-banana 3, nano-banana-2 6, nano-banana-2-lite 3, nano-banana-pro 9, GPT Image models 9; `pose_mode` on the nano-banana models fixes the aspect ratio to 3:4 and a posed `nano-banana` request is generated by `nano-banana-pro` (Pro plan or above), while `nano-banana-2`, `nano-banana-2-lite` and the GPT Image models keep the requested model; `generate_multi_view` and `aspect_ratio` cannot be combined; `image_urls` holds three URLs with multi-view, otherwise one.
- [Image to Image API](https://docs.meshy.ai/api/image-to-image): same `generate_multi_view`, `aspect_ratio` and `remove_background` rules; GPT Image models cost 12.
- [Image to 3D API](https://docs.meshy.ai/api/image-to-3d): `should_remesh` default false on Meshy 6 and 7; `target_polycount` 100 to 300,000 (default 30,000); `pose_mode`; `texture_prompt` max 800 characters.
- [Rigging API](https://docs.meshy.ai/api/rigging): 300,000-face limit for `input_task_id`; textured humanoid models only.
